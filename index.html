    26+ YEARS PRESERVING THE CLASSICAL SCHOOLS

    India's Living Miniature Art Heritage

    Led by senior artist Om Prakash Kumawat alongside artist Priyanka Kumawat, OM & PRIYANKA is an internationally celebrated atelier dedicated to the unbroken lineage of royal Indian miniature painting.

    From private presidential collections at Rashtrapati Bhavan and the Prime Minister’s Office to prestigious art collectors in London, New York, Paris and Tokyo, their masterworks revive the sacred alchemy of natural gemstone pigments, handmade Wasli folios, and 24K Hilkari gold illumination.

    A PLACE FOR PATIENCE

    Welcome to Our Jaipur Atelier

    Stone mortars grinding lapis lazuli and malachite, the fragrant scent of antique handmade Wasli paper, and the rhythmic stroke of a single-hair squirrel brush meeting the page in silence.

    For art connoisseurs, museums, interior designers, and international buyers, our Jaipur studio offers a direct, personal communion with India's sacred artistic genius. We welcome collectors for private viewings and curated commissions.
