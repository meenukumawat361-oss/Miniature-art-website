India's art heritage, carried forward

A tradition in every tiny detail.

Indian miniature painting is a heritage of intricate storytelling, luminous colour and remarkable detail. Each carefully painted line brings its history and artistry into the present.

OM & PRIYANKA is led by senior artist Om Prakash Kumawat, alongside artist Priyanka Kumawat. Together, they honour this enduring tradition through thoughtful, hand-painted art.

Paint, paper, pigment, and the gentle rhythm of a brush meeting the page. This is where senior artist Om Prakash Kumawat and artist Priyanka Kumawat honour Indian miniature-painting traditions through work made with patience and care.
