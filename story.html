    Where Centuries of Lineage Breathe Upon a Single Hair

    The life, devotion, and sacred alchemy of Senior Artist Om Prakash Kumawat and Artist Priyanka Kumawat. Preserving India's royal schools across Rashtrapati Bhavan, the PMO, and global collections.

    LIVING MASTER OF RAJASTHAN

    The Roots of Boraj & Guru-Shishya Parampara

    Born in 1986 in the historic village of Boraj near Jaipur, Om Prakash Kumawat has spent more than twenty-six years in quiet, transcendent dialogue with India’s greatest miniature painting traditions.

    Recognizing that miniature art is not merely an aesthetic pursuit but a spiritual tapasya (devotion), he surrendered himself completely to the sacred Guru-Shishya parampara of Jaipur, learning the ancient science of facial contouring (*chinnha-vidya*) and Hilkari gold levigation.

    Shri Rajesh Ji • Composition & Geometry

    Mastered the mathematical balance of royal durbar courts, elephant cavalries, and perspective.

    Shri Rajendra Gaid Ji • Mineral Extraction

    Unveiled the secrets of natural gemstone grinding, lapis lazuli levigation, and Hilkari gold.

    Shri R.K. Sharma Ji • Devotional *Bhava*

    Instilled the classical philosophy, emotional depth, and poetic lyricism that define Kangra and Mewar.

    "A small miniature takes months, but preparing the colors takes longer still. It demands complete surrender. You do not paint through glass; you hold your breath and steady your gaze until the universe appears without a single flaw."
    — Om Prakash Kumawat (Dainik Bhaskar Feature)
    MATERIALS UNTOUCHED BY TIME

    The Sacred Alchemy: From Earth to Eternity

    In an era of mass reproduction, Master Om Prakash Kumawat adheres strictly to the orthodox techniques practiced in royal karkhanas six centuries ago.

    ◈

    Handmade Wasli Paper

    Multiple layers of unbleached sunn-hemp and cotton rag paper fused with wheat starch, burnished for days with an agate stone (*gholta*) for a porcelain sheen.

    ✦

    100% Mineral Stones

    Raw lapis lazuli for ultramarine blues, malachite for deep jade greens, cinnabar (hinglu) for vermilion, and conch shell white ground in real seashells.

    ✒

    Single-Hair Squirrel Brush

    Hand-crafted brushes (*kalam*) bound inside bird quills, tapering to a single micro-point finer than human hair to execute the famed *pardaz* stippling.

    ★

    24K Hilkari Gold

    Pure twenty-four-karat gold leaf rubbed with gum arabic and honey into liquid gold powder, burnished with a heated agate needle for a permanent celestial gleam.

    Monumental Masterpiece

    The Court of Maharaja Ranjit Singh

    Five years in the making, depicting the historic Lahore Durbar with intricate gold canopies. Acquired by an international curator for over ₹1 Crore ($120,000+ USD).

    PROVENANCE & PRESTIGE

    Enshrined in India's Supreme Corridors

    Om Prakash Kumawat’s artworks have transcended private gallery walls to occupy pride of place within India’s national patrimony, greeting heads of state and global dignitaries.

    🏛

    Rashtrapati Bhavan (Presidential Palace, New Delhi)

    Permanent installation of classical Rajasthani miniature narratives within India’s supreme sovereign residence.

    ★

    Prime Minister’s Office (PMO, New Delhi)

    Specially selected works representing India’s cultural heritage presented to foreign dignitaries and visiting delegations.

    ◈

    Delhi Vidhan Sabha & Virasat-e-Khalsa

    Monumental historical commissions honoring Sikh history, Punjab court narratives, and constitutional heritage.

    PRESERVING THE FLAME

    Mentorship & The Next Generation

    For Master Om Prakash, mastery is a sacred responsibility to future generations. Alongside his daughter and protégé, talented artist Priyanka Kumawat, he trains apprentices and hosts international masterclasses.

    Together, they ensure that the knowledge of stone levigation, single squirrel hair brushes, and Wasli preparation continues to flourish in Jaipur and worldwide.
